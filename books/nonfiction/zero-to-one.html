Zero to One – 04/17/2026

Startup books are similar to self help ones. Easy to consume but difficult to turn into practice. Secrets do not need to be something grandiose and startups do not have to be a leap of faith. Startups are constrained games of creation. Learn how to create first then incorporate monetary incentive at least in my two cents.

Current secrets:

  • Answers readily come from a value proposition
    • Often simple ones e.g. catalogue thoughts on creating something
  • There are good returns to thinking about cold start / distribution
  • Machine learning or systems generally attract different groups of people
