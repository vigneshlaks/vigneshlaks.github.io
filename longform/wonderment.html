Wonderment

Wonderment defined through sea and stars. To experience. Consider an antagonistic perspective. Life's cartwheels limited by ambition and flexibility. Disquieting in their difficulty to forge. Each earning a stature. Theology: direction and morality. Philosophy: abstract and generative. Politics: authoritative and binding. History: patterns and characters. Plead or bleed for forgiveness. But act. Act with individualism. Act through and beyond diction. The antithesis is to savor in relinquishment or balk in normality. To elicit wonder implies movement. Life's trajectory is solidified alongside character, ours and others. The byproduct of subjected ideals or an envisioned character. Millennia of work to inspire. Spectacle is ephemeral without the timeless. Creative pursuits require breadth. Each bellow promotes greater depth. Wonderment is a life's directive.
