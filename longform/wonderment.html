Wonderment

Wonderment as it is traditionally defined establishes the importance of looking outwards. Towards the sea and stars and to experience. Consider the antagonistic perspective. A day becomes limited by ambition. This can be disquieting as ambition is difficult to forge. Is this position as unenviable as it has come to be considered?

The antithesis of wonderment is to savor in relinquishment or balk in normality. To elicit wonder implies movement. To give all and now, in haste. Plead or bleed for forgiveness. But above all act. Act with individualism. Act beyond diction. Life's trajectory is solidified alongside character, ours and others. An aggregated byproduct and the dredges of ideals or something else. Not better or worse just different.

Wonderment has been theorized, built, and architected. Wonderment is entwined with action. Millenia of work. Spectacle is ephemeral without its marriage with timeless qualities. Those within the past had room to breath. Creative pursuits require breadth. Each gentle bellow, another opportunity to engage with greater depth. Wonderment is a life's directive.
