Neural Nets

We are given a graph with input nodes x₁ and x₂ with the goal of producing output ŷ. We choose weights to multiply these inputs, depicted by edge weights. Consider the example where x₁ = 5, x₂ = 6, and ŷ = 12:

To find the weights, we solve: 5w₁ + 6w₂ = 12, giving us w₁ = 2 and w₂ = 7/6.

The network computes: y = x₁ · w₁ + x₂ · w₂ = 5 · 2 + 6 · 7/6 = 10 + 7 = 12
