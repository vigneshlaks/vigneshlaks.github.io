Sophie's World – 04/17/2026

The story is structured where a mysterious figure gives a series of notes to the protagonist. These notes are used to teach the audience about Greek philosophy. Some subtle fantastical elements are introduced as the story progresses. This is a good book to introduce to someone in their adolescence to spur more individual thinking. I did not enjoy it very much and had a hard time recalling the information though this may be because Greek philosophy is not very beginner friendly. Read about half before stopping.
