This morning I woke and yawned widely. The recognizable gait I've become accustomed to hearing approaches. An initial series of steps and a right bend followed by another harsh turn. I thud onto the cement block before the grass. As I have gotten a bit older, my joints have begun to fatigue. Walking carefully around the sweetgum balls barely perceptible as the sun comes out, I make my rounds.

Later in the day, I hear the clink of a metal collar and the atmosphere in the house changes. A shared giddiness and half-hearted attempts at maintaining composure as the collar is clasped on. The garage door scrolls open or the front door swings and a second rush of adrenaline overwhelms me. Even as the chiding grows louder it feels further as my attention is brought to my surroundings.

The carpeted texture of home instead asphalt, concrete, and gravel. The scent of turmeric and masala powder now a neutral one. A siren approaches and the ground tilts. Maybe another dog or even a wolf but more likely an ambulance. I echo the tune before continuing. The street is crossed to safety, the ditch leaped over. The grass is more malleable than it is at home. My attention flares as the fenced dogs a distance away bark in my direction. I hold back retorts to avoid being sent back home.
