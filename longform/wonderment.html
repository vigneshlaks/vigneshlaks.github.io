Wonderment

Wonderment defined through sea and stars. To experience. Consider an antagonistic perspective instead gleaned elsewhere. Hours of the day lay bare. The cartwheels of life limited by ambition and flexibility alone. Disquieting in its difficulty to forge. The antithesis of wonderment is to savor in relinquishment or balk in normality. To elicit wonder implies movement. Giving, in earnest, with urgency.

Each earning a stature. Theology: direction and morality. Philosophy: abstract and generative. Politics: authoritative and binding. History: patterns and characters. Plead or bleed for forgiveness. But above all act. Act with individualism. Act through and beyond diction. Life's trajectory is solidified alongside character, ours and others. An aggregated byproduct and subjected dredges of ideals or an envisioned character. Millennia of work to inspire. Spectacle is ephemeral without marriage to the timeless. Creative pursuits require breadth. Each bellow promotes greater depth. Wonderment is a life's directive.
