Wonderment

Wonderment traditionally defines looking outwards. Towards the sea and the stars. To experience. Consider an antagonistic perspective. Life limit's are defined by ambition. Disquieting as ambition is difficult to forge. Is this position as unenviable as it has come to be considered?

The antithesis of wonderment is to savor in relinquishment or balk in normality. To elicit wonder implies movement. To give all and now, in haste. Plead or bleed for forgiveness. But above all act. Act with individualism. Act beyond diction. Life's trajectory is solidified alongside character, ours and others. An aggregated byproduct and subjected dredges of ideals or something else. Not better or worse just different.

Wonderment is entwined with action. Millenia of work to inspire. Spectacle is ephemeral without a marriage with timeless qualities. Those before had room to breath. Creative pursuits require breadth. Each gentle bellow, used to engage with greater depth. Wonderment exists as a life's directive.
